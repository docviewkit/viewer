## Design Context

### Users
Software teams embed DocViewKit in web and desktop products to inspect, search, and navigate customer documents without uploading them to a conversion service. The Viewer should keep attention on the document and make frequent navigation controls immediately usable.

### Brand Personality
Professional, restrained, and precise. The interface should feel dependable and product-ready without competing with the host application or the document itself.

### Aesthetic Direction
A compact, quiet utility interface with clear hierarchy, neutral surfaces, and restrained blue accents. Support both light and dark chrome while preserving authored document colors. Avoid decorative effects, oversized controls, and consumer-app styling.

### Design Principles
- Give document content the largest practical share of the viewport.
- Keep common controls compact on pointer devices and comfortably targetable on touch devices.
- Preserve clear focus, hover, disabled, and responsive states.
- Prefer semantic tokens and bounded customization over one-off styling.
- Make every visible element serve document viewing, navigation, or trustworthy status feedback.
